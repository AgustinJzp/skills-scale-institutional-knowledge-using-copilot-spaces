# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA Lead

### Role Summary
QA Leads own the quality strategy, test planning, and defect management. They work closely with Developers and Product Managers to ensure acceptance criteria are met and features are ready for release.

### Responsibilities
- Define quality strategy and test plans
- Own test planning, execution, and defect triage
- Work with Developers to ensure test coverage and automation
- Coordinate manual and automated QA processes
- Validate that acceptance criteria are met before release
- Escalate critical quality issues and manage blockers

### Goals
- Ensure features meet quality standards and acceptance criteria
- Reduce production defects and customer-impacting issues
- Build confidence in release readiness through testing

### Interactions
- **Coordinates with Developers** for test coverage requirements and technical quality gates
- **Partners with Product Manager** for acceptance criteria validation and definition
- **Aligns with Project Manager** on release timelines and QA resource planning
- **Works with UX Designer** for usability testing and user acceptance validation

### Typical Communication
- QA test plans and status in sprint planning
- Defect reports and triage in daily standups
- Quality metrics and readiness assessments in release reviews

---

## UX Designer

### Role Summary
UX Designers ensure user experience best practices are followed throughout the product lifecycle. They conduct research, design workflows and mockups, and collaborate with Product Managers to validate requirements and usability.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and define user personas
- Design workflows, wireframes, and high-fidelity mockups
- Collaborate with Product Managers on feature requirements
- Validate solutions through user testing and feedback
- Ensure accessibility and usability standards are met
- Provide design guidance and feedback during development

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, accessible user experiences
- Reduce user confusion and support burden
- Build products that delight users and meet business goals

### Interactions
- **Collaborates with Product Manager** in early planning and requirement definition
- **Works with Developers** during implementation to ensure design fidelity
- **Partners with QA Lead** for usability testing and user acceptance validation
- **Engages Stakeholders** for design reviews and feedback

### Typical Communication
- Design mockups and prototypes in kickoff and planning
- Design review sessions and feedback loops
- Usability test results and insights in retrospectives

---

## Technical Writer

### Role Summary
Technical Writers document features, APIs, and processes. They maintain user guides, developer documentation, and process documentation. They work with Developers and Product Managers to ensure accuracy and clarity.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain user guides and help documentation
- Document APIs, SDKs, and technical integrations
- Write process documentation and runbooks
- Collaborate with Developers for technical accuracy
- Maintain a single source of truth for documentation
- Update documentation as features evolve

### Goals
- Enable users and developers to succeed with clear, accurate documentation
- Reduce support burden through comprehensive documentation
- Accelerate onboarding for new team members

### Interactions
- **Works with Developers** for technical content and API documentation
- **Collaborates with Product Manager** for product context and feature descriptions
- **Partners with QA Lead** for process documentation and test case clarity
- **Engages UX Designer** for user-facing documentation and terminology

### Typical Communication
- Documentation drafts and updates in sprint reviews
- Content accuracy reviews with Developers
- Documentation roadmap in planning meetings

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
Business Analysts clarify business requirements, analyze feasibility, and support backlog refinement. They bridge the gap between business needs and technical implementation, ensuring alignment on scope and priorities.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct requirement gathering and analysis
- Clarify business needs and define acceptance criteria
- Support backlog refinement and prioritization
- Analyze feasibility and identify technical and business constraints
- Document requirements and decision rationale
- Coordinate with stakeholders to ensure alignment

### Goals
- Ensure clear, actionable requirements that reduce rework
- Align business priorities with technical feasibility
- Reduce scope creep and miscommunication

### Interactions
- **Collaborates with Product Manager** for requirement gathering and business context
- **Works with Project Manager** for timeline feasibility and resource planning
- **Partners with Developers** to validate technical feasibility and identify constraints
- **Engages Stakeholders** to clarify business needs and priorities

### Typical Communication
- Requirement specifications and user stories in planning
- Feasibility assessments and trade-off analysis
- Business metrics and impact analysis in reviews
